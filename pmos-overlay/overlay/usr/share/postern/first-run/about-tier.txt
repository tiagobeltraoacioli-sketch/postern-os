================================================================
 About this tier -- Postern for postmarketOS (community edition)
================================================================

Read this once. We would rather tell you plainly than have you assume
something we did not promise.

WHAT THIS IS
------------
You installed Postern SOFTWARE on postmarketOS. This is the community
edition: the LOWER-TRUST, NON-ATTESTABLE tier.

It is NOT "Postern OS". It is always "Postern for postmarketOS".

  Postern software on postmarketOS. No reproducibility or attestation
  guarantee. The attestable images are "Postern OS" (Mobile NixOS) only.

NOT COVERED BY THE POSTERN SEAL
-------------------------------
The Postern Seal covers only the reproducible, verity-sealed Postern OS
(Mobile NixOS) images, on a shorter list of devices. This installation
carries no Seal and makes no attestation claim.

  - Packages are cryptographically SIGNED: you can verify who published
    them and that they were not tampered with in transit.
  - They are NOT reproducible: nobody -- including us -- can rebuild a
    bit-for-bit identical file, because postmarketOS/Alpine is not
    bit-reproducible as a distro property.
  - There is no hardware anchor: pmOS devices ship with an unlocked
    bootloader, so there is no device attestation here.

YOUR WALLET KEYS
----------------
Your wallet keys are stored in SOFTWARE, encrypted at rest (Argon2id).
There is NO hardware-backed keystore on this platform -- no TEE, no
StrongBox. Fine for daily use; not for keys you cannot afford to lose.

PRIVACY IS TRANSPARENT, NOT SECRET
----------------------------------
  - Network traffic routes through Tor using Arti (the Rust Tor stack):
    the node embeds it in-process, and an on-device SOCKS proxy on
    localhost:9150 serves the app suite. Config: /etc/postern/arti.toml.
  - A default-deny firewall (nftables) blocks unsolicited inbound
    traffic. Config: /etc/nftables.d/postern.nft.
  - NO covert telemetry. Nothing here phones home. Any future analytics
    will be OPT-IN and disclosed before a single byte leaves the device.

ABOUT THE COIN

Postern's revenue never touches the coin. Personal use is free.
