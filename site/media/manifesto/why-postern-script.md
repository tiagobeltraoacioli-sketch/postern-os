# Why Postern — manifesto film

**A ~95-second vision piece for the home page.** English narration + burned-in
subtitles. Grounded in the founder's essay *Pre-Commitment, Ulysses, and the
Cryptographic Constitution of the Twenty-First Century* (`docs/ENSAIO_2…`) —
freedom, property, life, privacy. It uses the enduring themes and deliberately
leaves out the (contested, product-layer) AML/CFT-enforcement material so the
public "no surveillance / ownerless" message stays clean.

> Production note: this repo does not render the final voiced .mp4. This file is
> the narration + storyboard; `why-postern.srt` is the subtitle track. Voice it
> (human or TTS), cut to the storyboard, burn in the SRT.

**Tone:** slow, grave, hopeful. Graphite + brass. No hype. No coins on screen,
ever. Type-driven; the gate glyph appears only at the open and close.
