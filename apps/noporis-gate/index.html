NoporIS — the guarded crossing Google-Play side · less private

A controlled span between two personas on one phone. Nothing crosses that you didn't hand across.

The Google-Play side is the less-private side, by construction. NoporIS sells separation and convenience — not privacy on that side. That persona runs Google Play Services and phones home to Google; NoporIS cannot change that. This is an app on stock Android — not an operating system, never “Postern OS”, and not a “Seal”. A rooted or compromised phone defeats the separation. Reference-prototype, UNAUDITED. The coin is worthless and is not a security. Personal use is free. This is not legal advice.
