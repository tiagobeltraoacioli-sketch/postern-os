Porog — the guarded door

A locked-down work profile you enroll yourself, on the phone you already own

A door, not a new phone

Porog self-enrolls a managed work profile on your stock, un-rooted Android. No root, no OS replacement, no unlock. Your wallet-side apps live behind that door; the rest of your phone is untouched.

Read this once, plainly. Porog is a key-attested device — not a measured-boot workspace. It attests the phone (its keystore, boot and patch state), and it is not a measured-boot workspace: it says nothing about the code or contents inside the profile. There is no Postern Seal, no measured boot, no os_roothash here — that belongs to Postern OS Mobile alone. A rooted or kernel-compromised host defeats the whole boundary. Hardened, not anonymous. Enroll the work profile

Android provisions a managed profile you own (ACTION_PROVISION_MANAGED_PROFILE). It stays on your device — no data leaves, nothing is sent to a server. You can wipe just this profile at any time, leaving the rest of the phone intact.

What the door locks down inside itself: screenshots, IME leakage, cross-profile clipboard, unknown-source installs — configured by the DPC, not by root.

    What it does NOT prove — ever
    • That the workspace ran unmodified — attestation measures the phone, not the code inside the profile.

    • That a rooted host is defeated — a kernel compromise crosses this boundary.
