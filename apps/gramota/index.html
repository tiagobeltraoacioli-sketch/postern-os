Onboard your organization

Gramota is a thin front over Postern's real license spine. It carries no crypto: it verifies who you are on-device, then asks /license/master to mint a vendor-signed master bundle you hold yourself.

Your entity

A D-U-N-S number is a 9-digit business identifier. The spine's D&B gate resolves it to a real entity; a garbage number is refused (422).

Only the DUNS crosses the wire — never documents.

Attestation

An authorized principal attests to the request. This is a declaration, not proof — the KYC ladder (next) is what raises assurance.

KYC — on-device Level of Assurance

Raise assurance without leaking anything. Each rung runs on this device; only a tiny non-PII verdict is attached to the issuance request.

What crosses the wire: { decision, assurance, loa, ref, checkedAt } plus, on the OCR rung, { ocrMatch, fieldsMatched }. Never document images, raw OCR text, names, or addresses. Network reach is CSP-pinned to connect-src 'self' — the same-origin /license/* endpoints only; no third-party origin is reachable.

Issue the master bundle

This POSTs your request to /license/master. The vendor signs the master with its private key (which never leaves the server) and returns a bundle that includes your org master private key.

Keep this private. The bundle below contains master_private_key_pkcs8 — your organization's signing key. It is returned once and is never stored on the server. Save it somewhere safe (a secret manager). Anyone with it can mint sub-accesses for your org. Gramota does not persist or transmit it.

Seats & devices — hand off to Consiglio

Gramota's job ends at issuance. Minting per-user / per-device sub-tokens and enforcing dual-control, separation-of-duty and access layers is Consiglio's job — it holds your master key locally and never phones home.

Master issued. Next: open the Postern Business console (apps/postern-enterprise/console.html) or Ezekiel 1 (apps/ezekiel1), paste the bundle, and mint signed sub-accesses offline. Both verify the same vendor → master → sub Ed25519 chain.
