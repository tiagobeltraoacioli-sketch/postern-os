Postern Labs · the guarded bridge · Kalinov crossing
Design note   NoporIS is the guarded bridge — a controlled crossing between two worlds that both keep existing: your personal side and a second persona (business, or a Google-Play-enabled side). It is a different shape from Porog, the guarded door into the hardened world. Door and bridge are two products that share one signer core and never merge their trust boundaries. Every mechanism below is either a shipped scaffold or a design spec — labelled as such — on stock Android, never "Postern OS".

One keyring. A door on one side, a bridge on the other.

Porog is a room you are in or out of — one threshold, sealed, Tor-only. NoporIS is a span you stand on to reach the other side, where both worlds keep running. You choose which side is live, and exactly what crosses.

The guarded crossing — what you hand across

◆ NOPORIS · LESS PRIVATE · NON-ATTESTABLE

Nothing crosses that you did not hand across. Every capability starts OFF (deny). Each is a lever you throw — visible, disclosed, individually revocable — mapping to noporis-gate's crossings.{clipboard,files,mic,camera,location} (each a per-capability {open, scope, openedAtIso} object). That is the guarded crossing, not a metaphor over an open door.

Clipboard (both directions)
Lets copy-and-paste cross between your personal side and the Google-Play side.
Risk: an app on the less-private side can read whatever you last copied — including passwords or wallet addresses. Auto-off when you step off the span.
Shared folder (personal → Google side)
Mounts one folder across. Only what you drop in Shared is visible; nothing else crosses.
Risk: every app on the far side can read that folder — not just the one you meant.
Microphone → Google side
Feeds a live mic across. An app with mic permission on the far side can listen live.
Risk: live audio into a non-attestable app that may phone home. Turn it on only while you need it.
Camera → Google side
Feeds a live camera across. An app with camera permission sees and can upload the video.
Risk: live video into apps that can upload it. Never leave on across sessions.
Location → Google side
Gives the far side your position. Choose coarse (city-level, the safer default) or precise.
Risk: precise location is a strong tracking signal to every app and its servers.
Each true value maps to concrete plumbing (clipboard → wl-clipboard; the rest → Waydroid/LXC device passthrough). A false or absent value crosses NOTHING. This page persists a switcher-local mock (flat booleans) to localStorage, all default DENY — it is not the gate-store state file. An on-device wrapper would map these fields onto noporis-gate's crossings.* per-capability objects ({open, scope, openedAtIso}), treating a missing field as deny. The kill-switch sets killSwitch.engaged — it stops the far side and never touches wallet state on the sealed base (sealedWallet.preserved pinned true).

The signer — two pairings, never one that spans the bridge

Can one paired signer authorize across the bridge? No — and it must not. One physical phone holds two independent pairings, one per world (each its own PairingRecord: peer pins + a forward-secret session_root). A grant issued to the Google-Play peer cannot auto-approve anything for the Porog peer — session binding fails closed. There is no persona field anywhere; a "world" is simply a distinct paired peer.

World-badged approval (WYSIWYS)

The phone recomputes the human sentence from the decoded bytes, never from wire text — and binds the world label to which pairing the frame arrived on, not to any field the requester controls. A compromised desktop can't narrate a lie, and now can't mislabel its world either. Consequence tier stays byte-derived: a transfer is a transfer in either world.

Anti-theater — stated on the surface, not only in a doc

  • !The bridge separates and conveniences; on the Google side it does not make you private — that persona phones home to Google.
  • !A rooted or kernel-compromised host defeats the separation. Both the managed-profile boundary and the Waydroid boundary are only as strong as the Android kernel + SELinux; a rooted/malware endpoint reads keys from RAM and can forge the approval UI.
  • !Software signing in RAM is not a hardware wallet. The real signer is mobile/core's WalletCore; the scaffolds' ScaffoldSigner is a deterministic test stand-in, not cryptography.
  • !Attestation here is key-attested (device), not measured-boot (workspace). No StrongBox claim on a device that only got TEE — the tier obtained is always named.
  • !This is an app on stock Android / the OS-desktop surface — it is not "Postern OS" and must not borrow its measured-boot assurances.

The story — kept neutral and honest

In Slavic cosmology two worlds coexist: Yav (the everyday, material world) and Nav (the other side). Between them runs the fire-river Smorodina, and the only way across is the Kalinov Bridge (Kalinov most) — a guarded crossing, held against all comers, never an open border. NoporIS is that bridge for one handset: it lets your two worlds meet and connect without a second phone, and like the Kalinov crossing the passage is controlled. That control is the product.

"Nopor" is a coined name — not a word or a portal from the tales. What is authentic is the crossing between two worlds; the myth is the narrative, not a claim of provenance. The classically named bridge is the Kalinov most; NoporIS is the chosen mark.

A neutral crossing, not a "world of the dead." Yav ↔ Nav is a passage between worlds that both keep existing — both of your worlds are alive and in use. That reading never appears in our copy or art.

The guardian is Yagabona — the coined mark that holds both the crossing and the door. We never use the registered "Baba Yaga" word mark.

Door + Bridge is the whole picture. Porog is the guarded door into the hardened world; NoporIS is the guarded bridge between two worlds that both persist. Two apps, two folklore shapes, one shared keyring.

Crossing to the Google-Play side

The far side is the less-private side. Here is exactly what that means, once — not a EULA wall.

It is non-attestable, by construction. A real Google-Android system in a sandboxed container. Sandboxed is not secure, private, or attested; tier = non-attestable, always. It is not covered by any Seal. Play Integrity fails by design, so banking / Google Wallet / most paid DRM will refuse or degrade.
It phones home to Google. This is a full Android userland; its apps reach the network and talk to Google and their own makers. NoporIS can't make an Android app private for you. What it can do: keep the far side off the base unless you hand a capability across (next), and drop the span whenever you want.
