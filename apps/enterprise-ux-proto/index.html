Enterprise + onboarding experience

From onboarding to access-release — one honest path

Three surfaces, one journey. An owner onboards the organization in Gramota, holds the org master key, then releases access to two separated consoles: Consiglio (identity, seats, dual-control) and Ezekiel 1 (access layers, clearance, Chinese walls). A wall stands between them: neither sees the other's private material, and neither reaches an employee's personal Postern identity.

Honesty — read this first

The honest labeling is the product. Nothing here overclaims.

●

Dual-control / SoD is enforced at runtime — in the service

The two-person rule (propose → a second, separated admin approves) is enforced by the host-run postern-consiglio-auth service: propose_change stages nothing, approve_change refuses self-approval and same-duty approval. These pages show that real gate; the in-page primitives are a browser stand-in.

◐

D&B (DUNS) gate is format-only until credentials

Today the gate is a 9-digit shape check — it does not yet resolve a DUNS to a real entity. That resolution (and the assurance it would carry) arrives only when D&B credentials are provisioned. The level-of-assurance stays honest about what has actually been verified.

◐

On-device OCR is the goal; manual entry is today

The privacy rail — OCR runs on the device, only a minimal non-PII verdict leaves it — is the design. Today's build takes what your on-device OCR read as manual input; a real build reads it from the camera/file. Either way, no document image or raw OCR text crosses the wire.

◼

Enforcement limits, stated

Key-withholding is hard denial for data at rest; in-session containment needs OS-level MAC (Postern OS machine profiles). A client-side page is advisory — it demonstrates and audits the model. No software stops a human photographing a screen.
