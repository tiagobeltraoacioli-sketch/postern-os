Onboard your organization

Gramota verifies who you are on this device, then mints a vendor-signed master you hold yourself. This prototype walks the whole flow — tier, DUNS, owner/manager attestation, the on-device KYC ladder, issuance, and the access-release to Consiglio and Ezekiel 1 — and is honest at every step about what is real and what is a stand-in.

D&B gate: format-only until credentials

Honesty: today the gate checks the 9-digit shape only — it does not yet resolve the DUNS to a real entity. Real resolution (and the assurance it carries) arrives when D&B credentials are provisioned; the level-of-assurance below stays honest until then.

Owner / manager attestation

An authorized principal — the owner or an empowered manager — attests to the request. This is a declaration, not proof; the KYC ladder next is what raises assurance.

KYC — on-device Level of Assurance

Raise assurance without leaking anything. Each rung runs on this device; only a tiny non-PII verdict is attached to the issuance request.

What crosses the wire: { decision, assurance, loa, ref, checkedAt } plus, on the OCR rung, { ocrMatch, fieldsMatched }. Never document images, raw OCR text, names, or addresses.

Access-release — hand off to Consiglio & Ezekiel 1

Gramota's job ends at issuance. You now release access to two separated consoles. You hold the master key; each console holds it locally to sign offline and never phones home. A Chinese wall stands between them.

Consiglio · identity & seats

  • Wallet-as-login — no passwords
  • Mints per-user / per-device sub-tokens
  • Dual-control / SoD: a change waits for a second, separated approver
  • Sees: enrollment + seat governance
chinese wall

Ezekiel 1 · access layers

  • Bell–LaPadula clearance floor
  • Brewer–Nash Chinese walls between conflict classes
  • Per-tenant isolation — one strand each
  • Sees: access-layer policy, not identity secrets
Chinese-wall visibility, honestly. Both consoles verify the same vendor → master → sub Ed25519 chain, but each sees only its own strand: Consiglio does not read Ezekiel's access-layer policy, Ezekiel does not read Consiglio's identity material, and neither reaches an employee's personal Postern identity — that stays out of the org's reach by construction. The org master private key never leaves your custody; the consoles hold it locally to sign, offline.
