Wallet-as-login & the two-person rule

Control of the wallet key is the login — no passwords, no shared secret. A privileged change then needs two separated people: one proposes, and a second, different admin whose duty is the Brewer–Nash counterpart approves. Neither can act alone.

1 · Sign in with your wallet

The server issues a single-use challenge; your wallet signs a domain-separated transcript with its hybrid Falcon-1024 ‖ ML-DSA-65 key; the server verifies it against your enrolled public key. The real verify runs in the service; here it is simulated and labeled.

no session

Anti-replay: the nonce is single-use and retired on consumption. The transcript is domain-separated (consiglio:wallet-login:v1), so a login signature cannot be replayed as a payment or across orgs.

2 · Admins & separated duties

IT-admin ⟂ Compliance-officer — the two duties that must BOTH sign off. Two IT-admins (or two compliance-officers, or a self-approval) can never commit a change.

3 · Propose a privileged change

A change (offboard a user, or enroll/rotate a wallet) is staged by one admin. Nothing is mutated — it waits for a second, separated approver. Only an admin may propose; a business role cannot.

Proposing acts as your live session identity. Sign in above first.

4 · Pending — awaiting a second, separated approver

This is the gate. Each change sits at 1 of 2 until the separated counterpart approves. Try to self-approve, or approve as the same duty, and watch it fail-closed — the store is untouched.

No pending changes. Sign in and propose one above.

5 · Attempt & audit trail

Every propose, refusal, and commit is recorded — with the exact reason the service would return.

What is real vs stand-in. The two-person rule, self-approval refusal, and separated-duty requirement are enforced at runtime by control.rs in the host-run service — propose_change stages nothing, approve_change returns DualControl on self-approval and SodViolation on same-duty. This page reproduces that gate as the design of record; its checks are a browser stand-in, and wallet signing is simulated (the real hybrid verify is in the service). UNAUDITED.
