# Acknowledgments & Homage

## Nós, o Povo — We, the People

*Homage · of the people, for the people — on liberty, privacy, and the guarantee
of fundamental rights.*

Three words open the Constitution of the United States — "We, the People" — and
with them something new entered the world: authority that flows not from a crown
or a conqueror, but from a people binding itself, in writing, to rules it agrees to
keep even when keeping them costs something. That is the oldest and deepest idea I
build on: a promise made durable, a pre-commitment that outlives the mood of the
moment.

The Fifth Amendment is where that promise becomes a guarantee you can stand on:
"No person shall … be deprived of life, liberty, or property, without due process
of law; nor shall private property be taken for public use, without just
compensation." Read it slowly. The State is bound. Property is not held at the
pleasure of power — it is secured by law, and by law that must follow process. From
that security grew the freedom to contract and to enterprise, the freedom of
atypical and innominate agreements, and, in our century, the freedom to write
software and to issue tokens that no sovereign owns.

And liberty is not only the freedom to act in the open; it is the security of a
private sphere. The right of the people to be secure in their persons, houses,
papers, and effects — the Fourth Amendment's guarantee against unreasonable search
and seizure — is the quiet precondition of every other freedom: there is no free
conscience, no free enterprise, and no honest contract without a space the State
cannot enter without cause and without process. This is why I build Postern.
Privacy is not a feature to be bolted on; it is a fundamental right, and the tools
that protect it are, at their best, instruments of ordered liberty — the private
sphere defended by mathematics, as the Constitution defends it by law.

I do not treat these as abstractions. Constitutionalism, federalism, substantive
due process of law, and the guarantees of life, liberty, and property are the
load-bearing beams of the freer world we inherited. I honor the jurists who gave
that order its voice — Oliver Wendell Holmes Jr. and Felix Frankfurter among them —
and the long tradition of ordered liberty they served.

This is also the thread of my own scholarship. In *The Cryptographic Constitution*
I argue that a protocol can do, by mathematics, what a constitution does by law:
secure cooperation through strict pre-commitment and repeated games — civil
cooperation without a sovereign. The ownerless Bloch base is that idea made to run:
rules that bind because no one can bend them, the way the Fifth Amendment binds
because a people chose to be bound. What America wrote in law, we try to keep faith
with in code.

So Postern chooses its home, and says so plainly: **Postern is and will be
American** — in its allegiance to these principles first, and in the ground it
stands on. Not because it is fashionable, but because this is the constitutional
order that made building at all possible, and gratitude should be spoken out loud.

It is, in the end, a beautiful story — the one called the United States of America:
a people who wrote down their promises and then, imperfectly and across centuries,
kept trying to honor them. Of the people, for the people.

God bless America. *Deus abençoe a América* — em profundo respeito, admiração e
deferência à formação e à garantia dos direitos fundamentais da sociedade do
século XXI.

— T.T., founder

constitutional order, and to the institutions and people that made a freer world
possible.

and **Felix Frankfurter** among them — and the long tradition of ordered liberty
they served.

stands on the shoulders of others, and says so plainly.

In homage to all of this, **Postern is and will be American** — in its allegiance to
these principles, and in the home it chooses.

God bless America. *Deus abençoe a América* — em profundo respeito, admiração e

## With respect for the works and licenses we build upon

Postern is a builder among builders. We use others' work under their terms, name
them, and never pretend their work is ours:

- **Android Open Source Project (AOSP)** — used under the **Apache License 2.0**;
  attribution and NOTICE preserved. Postern products that run on Android are apps on
  stock Android; they are **never** called "Android," nor a fork of it, nor "Postern
  OS."
- **Google Play & Google Mobile Services (GMS)** — Google Play Services are **not**
  redistributed by Postern; where a Google-enabled persona exists it is the user's
  own Google account and install, honestly labeled the **less-private** side (it
  phones home to Google — that is separation, not privacy). We do **not** circumvent
  SafetyNet / Play Integrity; we disclose attestation honestly instead. The
  **"Android"** and **"Google Play"** names appear only in **nominative** use to say
  what our software interoperates with, never to imply endorsement.
- **Anthropic** — the Claude tooling used to build much of this. Where our apps talk
  to Anthropic, it is with the user's own key, and that path is labeled the
  least-private one.
- **The open-source ecosystem** — the Rust, Nix, and web crates and tools we depend
  on, each under its own license, listed with the products that use them.

Everything here remains **UNAUDITED, reference-prototype** software; the coin is
worthless by design and is not a security; the Bloch-SIS-PoW base is ownerless.
None of the respect recorded above is a claim of endorsement by any party named.
